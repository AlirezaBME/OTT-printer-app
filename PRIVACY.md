# LBP OTG Print privacy policy

Effective: October 3, 2026.

LBP OTG Print processes documents and printer information on your Android device. The app has no Internet permission, analytics, advertising SDK, account system or cloud backend. The app developer does not receive your documents or printer information automatically.

When you choose or share a PDF/image, the app copies it to private temporary storage to preview or prepare printing. Temporary copies are removed when replaced or closed, and Android may clear the cache. Process interruption can leave temporary cache files until a later cleanup or Android cache removal. Cloud backup is disabled. You can remove all stored app data using Android Settings or uninstall the app.

USB permission is requested only for a printer you connect. Diagnostic reports can include device names, manufacturer/model, USB vendor/product IDs, serial numbers, supported languages, status and local technical logs. Reports are exported only when you choose Copy/Share/Export. Share recipients receive the selected file. PCL/Canon stream exports contain rendered document content. Raw PRN import creates an unchanged private snapshot. Session metadata can include the job size, SHA-256 and first/last 64 bytes. Optional binary capture, disabled by default, records the complete printer job and known USB OUT/IN bytes. These files can contain document data and are shared only when you explicitly export a session trace.

When you use Android Print / Save PDF, the Android print system and the print service/destination you select process the output. Their privacy practices apply to their processing. A service may send documents over a network even though this app cannot access the Internet itself.

The app does not request broad storage, contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photo-library access. Android's document/photo picker grants access to the specific item you select.

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through https://github.com/AlirezaBME/OTT-printer-app/issues. Updates to this policy will be published in this repository.
